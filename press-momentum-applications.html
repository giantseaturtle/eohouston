EO Houston Opens Final Month of Applications for Momentum, a Two-Year Accelerator for Houston Business Owners

Applications close September 30 for the cohort beginning in October. The program targets owners between $250,000 and $1 million in revenue who are ready to scale past seven figures.

HOUSTON - EO Houston, the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, has opened the final month of applications for Momentum, its two-year accelerator for business owners who are past the startup stage but not yet past $1 million in revenue. Applications close September 30, 2026, interviews run through August and September, and the new cohort begins in October.

Momentum is built for a specific kind of Houston founder: an owner or co-founder with yearly revenue between $250,000 and $1 million and the ambition to pass $1 million in under two years. Members get eight months of classes a year, a confidential peer Forum that meets monthly year-round, and a social calendar that includes the chapter’s annual gala and retreats.

The program is an on-ramp into the Entrepreneurs’ Organization itself. Founded in 1987, EO is a global, peer-to-peer network of business owners across 200+ chapters in over 80 countries, built on a simple idea: entrepreneurs learn best from other entrepreneurs who have already lived the problem they are facing. EO Houston brings that idea to more than 200 member businesses, and Momentum extends it to the founders coming up behind them.

“The founders we built this for are already succeeding. They are just doing it the hard way, alone. Momentum gives them a curriculum, a Forum of peers with skin in the game, and direct access to Houston owners who have already made the jump past $1 million.”

Al Danto, Program Director, EO Houston Momentum

The curriculum was refined by Al Danto, an entrepreneur who built and exited his own company and is now a Partner at Exit Advisors and a Senior Lecturer in Entrepreneurship at Rice University, with input from Jackie Fisher, a Rice alum and owner of Houston’s 3 Men Movers.

Program dues are $3,500 for year one and $3,000 for year two, plus a one-time $500 initiation fee. The program takes no equity. Prospective members can review the full curriculum, dates, and FAQ and apply at eohouston.com/momentum.

About the Momentum program

Momentum is a two-year growth program of EO Houston for business owners and co-founders between $250,000 and $1,000,000 in yearly revenue who are ready to push past the $1 million mark. Members get eight months of classes a year taught around real operating experience, a confidential Forum of six to ten fellow owners that meets monthly year-round, and the chapter’s social calendar, including the annual gala and retreats. Full curriculum, dues, admissions dates, and the application are at eohouston.com/momentum.

About EO Houston

EO Houston is the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, a peer community of more than 200 member businesses run by owners who have scaled past $1 million in revenue, with 75+ chapter events a year. Learn more at eohouston.com.

About the Entrepreneurs’ Organization

Founded in 1987, the Entrepreneurs’ Organization is a global, peer-to-peer network of business owners across 200+ chapters in over 80 countries. EO exists for one reason: to help entrepreneurs learn and grow from each other, through confidential peer Forums, shared experience rather than advice, and a community that treats the whole entrepreneur, business and life, as one story. Momentum is an independent program of EO Houston. Rice University faculty and alumni participate as instructors and coaches in their individual capacities.
