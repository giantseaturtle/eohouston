Before you upload

  • Send traffic to eohouston.com/momentum or eohouston.com/join. The grant is tied to the eohouston.com domain, which is why the Momentum program has its own page here.
  • Grant-funded Performance Max serves on Search and Maps only, not Display or YouTube, so these run in fewer placements than a paid campaign would use.
  • Performance Max is exempt from the Ad Grants 5% click-through minimum.
  • Every asset group needs at least one logo asset or it will not publish. Use the white-ground logo unless the placement sits on a light background.
  • The extra-wide 3.2:1 files are not a Performance Max slot. They are for email headers, site banners, and social covers.
