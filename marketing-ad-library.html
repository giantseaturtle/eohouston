Before you upload

  • Send traffic to eohouston.com/momentum or eohouston.com/membership. The grant is tied to the eohouston.com domain, which is why the Momentum program has its own page here.
  • Grant-funded Performance Max serves on Search and Maps only, not Display or YouTube, so these run in fewer placements than a paid campaign would use.
  • Performance Max is exempt from the Ad Grants 5% click-through minimum.
  • Every asset group needs at least one logo asset or it will not publish. Square 1:1 is required; the wide 4:1 is optional. Logos are the one asset type where brand text is expected, so these carry the EO Houston lockup.
  • Which sizes Performance Max actually takes: landscape 1.91:1 and square 1:1 are required, portrait 4:5 is optional and unlocks mobile-first inventory. Max 20 of each, 5 MB per file. Those are the only three image slots it has.
  • The skinnier files are for other channels: 9:16 is Demand Gen and YouTube Shorts, 1:2 is the Display half-page, and 3.2:1 is for email headers, site banners, and social covers. None of them upload to a Performance Max asset group.
  • Google crops toward the centre, so keep judging these on the middle 80%. Every layout here already keeps the rings mark and faces inside that safe area.
  • No image in this library contains a child. Please keep it that way if you add more.
