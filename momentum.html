What is EO Momentum?

This is not a seminar. It's not fluff.

If you're still stuck doing everything yourself, putting out daily fires, and hitting a wall with growth, Momentum is the program designed to change that. In-person classes in the heart of Houston's entrepreneurial community, plus forums, coaching, and high-impact networking.

  • Escape the chaos and finally build systems that scale.
  • Trade hustle for structure, and create a business that runs without you.
  • Build a team that's aligned, accountable, and actually gets stuff done.
  • Master your numbers so you can lead with confidence and clarity.
  • Evolve into the next-level leader your business demands.

The Momentum journey

A powerful three-part path to scale

World-class education, peer-to-peer learning in Forum, and deep community engagement at EO Houston events.

1

Education

An 8-month course each year where you work on your business, not just in it. Sessions led by seasoned coaches and faculty alongside accomplished EO Houston entrepreneurs. Year 1 ends with a custom Operating Manual for your business; Year 2 turns plans into execution.

2

Forum

A confidential, peer-led group of 6-8 entrepreneurs from non-competing businesses, guided by trained EO Houston member coaches. Your personal advisory board, meeting monthly to tackle your hardest business and personal challenges.

3

Events

Regular EO Houston chapter learning and social events that expand your network and sharpen your business knowledge, so you grow as a business, family, and community leader.

Qualifications & investment

Do you qualify?

You're an owner or co-founder of a business with yearly revenue over $250,000, with the drive to build a sustainable company generating more than $1,000,000 in less than two years. You're likely a fit if you're:

  • Wearing every hat and feeling burned out.
  • Struggling to delegate or trust your team.
  • Growing, but unsure how to level up your leadership.
  • Craving a tribe of smart, driven peers who get it.
