What is EO Momentum?

This is not a seminar. It's not fluff.

The Momentum journey

A powerful three-part path to scale

World-class education, peer-to-peer learning in Forum, and deep community engagement at EO Houston events.

1

Education

An 8-month course each year where you work on your business, not just in it. Sessions led by seasoned coaches and faculty alongside accomplished EO Houston entrepreneurs, structured like a collaborative MBA-style class: you prepare ahead of each session, then apply the tools directly to your real business between sessions.

2

Forum

A confidential, peer-led group of 6-8 entrepreneurs from non-competing businesses, guided by trained EO Houston member coaches. Your personal advisory board, meeting monthly for four-hour sessions to tackle your hardest business and personal challenges.

3

Events

The fun part. Chapter socials, learning luncheons, the annual gala, and retreats that expand your network and sharpen your business knowledge, so you grow as a business, family, and community leader.

The Year One curriculum

Eight months that build on each other

Drawn from the EO Momentum Year One Field Guide. By the end of Year 1 you'll have a fully customized Operating Manual for your company: core processes, values, financial dashboards, and accountability structures. In Year 2 you keep your Forum group and monthly coaching and restart the curriculum with a new lens, alongside participating EO members, applying the scaling steps to a much more mature business and refining everything you built.

Month 1

Kick-Off & Foundations

Prioritize your workload and spot a revenue stream you could turn recurring.

Month 2

Unlocking Your Entrepreneurial DNA

Use your leadership profile to make a real delegation decision.

Month 3

Introduction to EOS: The Vision Component

Draft the Vision side of your V/TO, Core Values, Core Focus, 10-Year Target, and 3-Year Picture.

Month 4

Planning: From Vision to Traction

Turn your annual vision into 90-day Rocks with clear owners.

Month 5

Marketing: Position, Story, Amplify

Write a real positioning statement and find your differentiators.

Month 6

Right People, Right Seats

Architect a role the right way before you ever review a resume.

Month 7

Finance Day: The Founder's Financial Playbook

Calculate your Cash Conversion Cycle and pick the KPIs that actually matter.

Month 8

Financing Your Growth: Debt vs. Equity

Figure out what fits your business and draft a fundable capital ask.

Classes meet in person once a month, typically the second Tuesday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM CT, at The Ion and local corporate offices across Houston.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Momentum, and who is it for?

Momentum is a two-year business accelerator for growth-minded Houston founders. It is built for owners generating roughly $250,000 to $1,000,000 in gross revenue who are ready to push past the $1M mark and are done trying to figure everything out alone. If you are ready to commit to the deep work, and you are based in or able to travel to Houston, this is for you.

What is the goal of the program?

To help you break through the operational ceilings that keep owners stuck in day-to-day firefighting, and to build the team, systems, and confidence to scale past seven figures. A core goal is to graduate you to $1M+ in revenue and into EO Houston, the EO chapter for business owners running companies above $1M. Fewer than 5% of businesses ever reach that level, and Momentum is built to get you there with a business that can sustain long-term growth.

How is the program structured?

It runs like a structured, collaborative accelerator, closer to an MBA-style class than a seminar. You prepare ahead of each session with assigned readings and reviews, then apply the tools directly to your real business between sessions. Year 1 focuses on foundational structures, processes, and leadership. In Year 2 you keep your Forum group and monthly coaching and go through the curriculum again with a new lens, alongside participating EO members, applying the scaling steps to a much more mature business and refining everything you built.

Why go through the same curriculum twice?

The classes repeat the way annual planning repeats. The material is your business, and your business is different every year.

In year one you set your vision, pick your 90-day priorities, design your next hire, and work your numbers. In year two you do all of it again, and every answer comes out different, because you are a year bigger and a year smarter. That is how growing companies work: the planning repeats, but the business never does.

And if you pass $1 million before year two ends, you graduate into EO Houston early. Year two is never a gate. It is the fastest path there.

What happens after the two years?

You can stay in Momentum for the full two years, or join EO Houston sooner if you qualify and continue the program from there. After two years, remaining in Momentum for one extra year is contingent on traction in your business and a realistic path to joining EO Houston. Either way, participants come out of the program much stronger, with stronger profits, growth, and momentum.

When and how often do classes meet?

Classes meet in person once a month over an 8-month curriculum, typically on the second Tuesday of the month from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, culminating in a graduation celebration. Your Forum group continues meeting monthly year-round, independent of the class calendar.

Where are the sessions held?

Sessions are hosted across Houston. Core meetings use dedicated spaces at The Ion, and select sessions take place at local corporate offices to give you direct exposure to a range of businesses and operating environments.

What are the revenue requirements to join?

Your business should have gross revenue between $250,000 and $1,000,000 for the most recent fiscal year. Revenue is documented through a program verification form as part of the application.

What will I walk away with?

By the end of Year 1 you will have built a fully customized Operating Manual for your company. It maps your core processes, organizational values, financial dashboards, and accountability structures, so you leave with a real system to run and scale the business, not just notes.

Is this just material I could get from a few business books?

No. Momentum draws on the best proven entrepreneurial operating and financial frameworks, but it is not a reading list. The value is in facilitated application, expert instruction, peer accountability, and a manual built specifically for your company. You are not reading about other people's businesses, you are building the systems for yours alongside a committed cohort of Houston founders.

Is Momentum a place to sell my services to other members?

No. Momentum and EO Houston operate under a strict non-solicitation policy. This is not a networking or lead-generation group, and members do not pitch, sell, or market services to one another or to the chapter. The group runs on EO's Gestalt experience-sharing protocol, where members speak only from their own personal experience rather than giving advice. That keeps the room a safe, honest place to work through real challenges.

How selective is admission?

Admission is selective and the cohort is limited. Every applicant completes an application and a personal interview, so we can make sure Momentum is the right fit for you and for the group. It is a two-way decision, not just yours.
