Built by Operators: An Exited Founder and the Owner of 3 Men Movers Shaped the Curriculum Behind Houston's Momentum Accelerator

Al Danto exited his own company before refining Momentum's two-year curriculum. Jacky Fischer, Rice alum and owner of 3 Men Movers, gave input from the operator's chair.

HOUSTON - The curriculum behind Momentum, EO Houston’s two-year accelerator for business owners heading toward $1 million in revenue, was not written by consultants. It was refined by Al Danto, an entrepreneur who built and exited his own company and is now a Partner at Exit Advisors and a Senior Lecturer in Entrepreneurship at Rice University, with input from Jacky Fischer, a Rice alum and the owner of Houston’s 3 Men Movers.

That distinction, real experience instead of theory, is the program’s core claim. Classes run eight months a year for two years and are taught around what has actually worked inside operating companies: building systems, hiring and leading a team, and turning an annual vision into 90-day commitments with clear owners.

“Everything in this curriculum got tested inside a real company, either mine or one run by the people who shaped it. If a tool did not survive contact with a real business, it did not make the cut.”

Al Danto, Program Director, EO Houston Momentum

The same philosophy runs through the program’s parent organization. The Entrepreneurs’ Organization, founded in 1987 and now spanning 200+ chapters in over 80 countries, is built on peer-to-peer experience-sharing: owners learning from owners who have already made the mistakes. Momentum members plug into EO Houston’s community of more than 200 businesses that have scaled past $1 million.

Momentum is an independent program of EO Houston. Rice University faculty and alumni participate as instructors and coaches in their individual capacities. Applications for the October 2026 cohort close September 30, 2026, at eohouston.com/momentum

About the Momentum program

Momentum is a two-year growth program of EO Houston for business owners and co-founders between $250,000 and $1,000,000 in yearly revenue who are ready to push past the $1 million mark. Members get eight months of classes a year taught around real operating experience, a confidential Forum of six to ten fellow owners that meets monthly year-round, and the chapter’s social calendar, including the annual gala and retreats. Full curriculum, dues, admissions dates, and the application are at eohouston.com/momentum

About EO Houston

EO Houston is the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, a peer community of more than 200 member businesses run by owners who have scaled past $1 million in revenue, with 75+ chapter events a year. Learn more at eohouston.com

About the Entrepreneurs’ Organization

Founded in 1987, the Entrepreneurs’ Organization is a global, peer-to-peer network of business owners across 200+ chapters in over 80 countries. EO exists for one reason: to help entrepreneurs learn and grow from each other, through confidential peer Forums, shared experience rather than advice, and a community that treats the whole entrepreneur, business and life, as one story. Momentum is an independent program of EO Houston. Rice University faculty and alumni participate as instructors and coaches in their individual capacities.
