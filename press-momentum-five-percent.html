Fewer Than 5 Percent of Businesses Ever Reach $1 Million in Revenue. A Houston Nonprofit Built a Two-Year Program to Change That

EO Houston's Momentum program gives owners stuck between $250,000 and $1 million a structured path across the gap most businesses never close.

HOUSTON - Most businesses that survive their first years still never cross $1 million in revenue. Fewer than 5 percent ever reach that level. Momentum, a two-year program run by EO Houston, the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, exists to move Houston owners across that line, and its application window for the October 2026 cohort closes September 15.

The gap between $250,000 and $1 million is where most growing companies stall, and the reasons repeat: the owner is still the product, the salesperson, and the back office at once, and there is no system that runs without them. Momentum’s curriculum, taught across eight months of classes a year for two years, is aimed at exactly that transition, from a business that depends on its owner to a company with systems, a team, and a plan measured in 90-day increments.

“Almost every owner who stalls between $250,000 and $1 million stalls for the same reason. The business still depends on them for everything. The curriculum is built to break that dependence, system by system.”

Al Danto, Program Director, EO Houston Momentum

The other half of the program is borrowed directly from what the Entrepreneurs’ Organization has practiced since 1987: the Forum, a small, confidential group of six to ten fellow owners who meet monthly year-round. Forum works on experience-sharing rather than advice. Members do not tell each other what to do; they tell each other what actually happened when they faced the same decision. Across EO’s 200+ chapters in over 80 countries, members consistently call Forum the single most valuable part of EO.

Momentum members also get direct access to EO Houston’s network of more than 200 businesses that have already scaled past $1 million. Applications for the cohort beginning October 2026 close September 15, 2026, at eohouston.com/momentum.

About the Momentum program

Momentum is a two-year growth program of EO Houston for business owners and co-founders between $250,000 and $1,000,000 in yearly revenue who are ready to push past the $1 million mark. Members get eight months of classes a year taught around real operating experience, a confidential Forum of six to ten fellow owners that meets monthly year-round, and the chapter’s social calendar, including the annual gala and retreats. Full curriculum, dues, admissions dates, and the application are at eohouston.com/momentum.

About EO Houston

EO Houston is the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, a peer community of more than 200 member businesses run by owners who have scaled past $1 million in revenue, with 75+ chapter events a year. Learn more at eohouston.com.

About the Entrepreneurs’ Organization

Founded in 1987, the Entrepreneurs’ Organization is a global, peer-to-peer network of business owners across 200+ chapters in over 80 countries. EO exists for one reason: to help entrepreneurs learn and grow from each other, through confidential peer Forums, shared experience rather than advice, and a community that treats the whole entrepreneur, business and life, as one story. Momentum is an independent program of EO Houston. Rice University faculty and alumni participate as instructors and coaches in their individual capacities.
