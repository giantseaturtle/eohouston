No Equity, No Catch: Houston's Momentum Accelerator Charges $3,500 a Year and Leaves Founders Owning Everything

While most accelerators trade access for ownership, EO Houston's nonprofit model means Momentum members keep every share of the companies they built.

HOUSTON - Accelerators usually come with a price measured in ownership: a stake in the company in exchange for the program. Momentum, the two-year accelerator run by EO Houston, takes a different route. Dues are $3,500 for year one, $3,000 for year two, and a one-time $500 initiation fee. No equity, no revenue share, no catch.

The model works because of who runs it. EO Houston is the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, a global nonprofit founded in 1987 that exists to help entrepreneurs learn and grow from each other. The chapter’s more than 200 member businesses are run by owners who already crossed the $1 million mark; Momentum is how the chapter extends its Forum model, curriculum, and community to the founders still making the climb.

“We do not want a piece of anyone’s company. EO Houston is a nonprofit. The point of Momentum is more Houston businesses crossing $1 million, still fully owned by the people who built them.”

Al Danto, Program Director, EO Houston Momentum

What members get for those dues runs two full years: eight months of classes a year built on real operating experience, a confidential Forum of six to ten peers that meets monthly year-round, and the chapter’s social calendar, from the annual gala to retreats. The program targets owners and co-founders between $250,000 and $1 million in yearly revenue who are ready to pass $1 million within two years.

Applications for the cohort beginning October 2026 close September 30, 2026. Curriculum, dues, dates, and the application are at eohouston.com/momentum.

About the Momentum program

Momentum is a two-year growth program of EO Houston for business owners and co-founders between $250,000 and $1,000,000 in yearly revenue who are ready to push past the $1 million mark. Members get eight months of classes a year taught around real operating experience, a confidential Forum of six to ten fellow owners that meets monthly year-round, and the chapter’s social calendar, including the annual gala and retreats. Full curriculum, dues, admissions dates, and the application are at eohouston.com/momentum.

About EO Houston

EO Houston is the Houston chapter of the Entrepreneurs’ Organization, a peer community of more than 200 member businesses run by owners who have scaled past $1 million in revenue, with 75+ chapter events a year. Learn more at eohouston.com.

About the Entrepreneurs’ Organization

Founded in 1987, the Entrepreneurs’ Organization is a global, peer-to-peer network of business owners across 200+ chapters in over 80 countries. EO exists for one reason: to help entrepreneurs learn and grow from each other, through confidential peer Forums, shared experience rather than advice, and a community that treats the whole entrepreneur, business and life, as one story. Momentum is an independent program of EO Houston. Rice University faculty and alumni participate as instructors and coaches in their individual capacities.
