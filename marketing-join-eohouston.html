All 4 carousels

    Email templates (backup, general invite)

    Four angles for inviting a qualified founder to join EO Houston. Each copies with formatting and images intact, ready to paste straight into Gmail.

    How to use these: click "Copy email" on whichever angle fits, then paste (Cmd/Ctrl + V) directly into a new Gmail draft, formatting, images, and links come along. Swap in the real name, tweak a line or two to sound like you, and send.
    • Member referral angle, written peer-to-peer, for a founder you know personally who’s never heard of EO.
    • Not at $1M yet angle, redirects a promising founder who doesn’t qualify yet toward EO Momentum, the on-ramp.
    • Chapter Director angle, written in Jennifer McClanahan’s voice, for a warm, official-feeling invite.
    • Global network angle, leads with the scale of EO worldwide for founders who care about that kind of access.

    Member referral angle

    Peer to peer

    Written from one member to a founder they know, personally recommending EO.

    SubjectThought of you, this has been huge for my business

    Hi [Name],

    I wanted to tell you about EO Houston, a community I’m part of for founders and owners of $1M+ companies. It’s not a networking group or a lead source, there’s a strict non-solicitation policy, so nobody’s selling. It’s a community built to make you a better leader and your business stronger.

    The best part is your own Forum, a confidential group of 6–10 fellow owners who meet monthly and become your most trusted personal board of advisors. On top of that, there are 75+ events a year and access to 19,000+ members across 200+ chapters worldwide.

    You’re likely a fit if:

    • You’re the founder, co-founder, owner, or controlling shareholder of your company
    • Your business grosses at least $1 million in annual revenue
    • You’re ready to share openly and give back, not sell or be sold to

    Take a look: eohouston.com/join, or I’m happy to just talk you through it.

    [Your name]

    Redirect to Momentum

    For a promising founder under $1M in revenue, points them to EO Momentum as the on-ramp.

    SubjectA two-year on-ramp into EO Houston

    If your business is between $250K and $1M, Momentum is a two-year accelerator built to help you scale past seven figures, build real operating systems, and grow into EO Houston membership. It’s run by EO Houston members and taught in partnership with Rice University’s Lilie Lab.

    Take a look: eomomentum.com, or learn more about the chapter it leads into at eohouston.com
